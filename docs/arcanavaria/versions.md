# Versions

Arcana Varia is made for **NeoForge 26.1.2** with **Thaumaturge 1.1.0** or newer. The whips also need
**Fundamentum 1.22.1** or newer.

## Changelog

**0.7.5**

- JEI support: info pages for the Forsaken Laboratory blocks, Laboratory Journals, Thurible blueprints and the
  Warden's Amber Heart.

**0.7.4**

- New textures drawn on vanilla art, including a new side for the Vis Lift.

**0.7.3**

- Arcana Varia's code and Oculifelis's own art are now LGPL-3.0-or-later. Earlier versions were All Rights Reserved.
- Tested: a full tower climb from the door to the vault, and all four whips.

**0.7.2**

- A Greatwood Warden fight saved and reloaded, even during its finale, carries on where it left off.
- Rune locks keep their progress more reliably.

**0.7.1**

- Wizard Towers and Forsaken Laboratories now spawn in the world: 4 tower shapes, over 200 palettes and 40 kinds of lab.
- Warded hatches between tower floors, shut until the floor below is cleared.
- Spawners inside a ward can be broken.
- Thurible blueprints and Blueprint Scrolls in structure loot.

**0.7.0**

- The Thurible, with 18 aspect clouds and three blueprints.
- The Forsaken Laboratory blocks: the ward, aspect and rune locks, beams and mirrors, vis lifts, phase stones, guardian
  altars with seven guardian kinds and 13 traits, and journals with hidden research.
- The Architect's Lens and Blueprint Scrolls.
- Needs Thaumaturge 1.1.0.

**0.4.0**

- The Alembic Lattice: intakes, pipes, outlets, junctions, synchronizers and splitter prisms.

**0.3.1**

- The whips coil in hand. Needs Fundamentum 1.22.1.

**0.3.0**

- Four whips, with Fundamentum.

**0.2.0**

- The Greatwood Warden, the Heartseed and the Warden's Amber Heart.

**0.1.0**

- Thaumium, Void and Elemental Scythes, and the Soul Devourer, Grim Harvest, Eclipse and Reaper's Step enchantments.
