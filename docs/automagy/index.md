# Automagia

Build a magical workshop with redstone devices, sorting chests, mirrors, essentia machines and an Inventarium storage network. Automagia brings Tuhljin's Automagy designs to [Thaumaturge](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1628024), with new code and art.

**Requires** Thaumaturge 1.1.0 or newer. [Public downloads](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1796732)

| 1.21.1 | NeoForge 21.1.259 or newer | 0.8.7 |

## In the workshop

- Magic Hourglasses, Redcrystals, Remote Comparators, Vis Readers and Tally Boxes control machines with redstone.
- Greedy, Eager and Tenacious Chests, runic lists and Hungry Hand Mirrors move and sort items.
- Thirsty Tanks and glyphs handle liquids. The Alchemical Boiler turns liquids into essentia, and the Essentia Locus and Aggregator move it.
- The Inventarium links archives, Unseen Scribes, a Scrivener's Oculus and a Requisition Tome into a storage network.
- Golem tools, the Golem's Workbench and the Nethermind add workshop jobs and rituals. The Thaumonomicon research tab explains how to build and use them.

The crafting focus uses Thaumaturge spell parts. Golem work uses seals and links, and wand settings replace the original device screens. The original amplified redcrystal signal behavior, Avarice Pearl enderman spawn boost and staff page are not reproduced.

With [JEI](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/238222), Thaumaturge shows the ordinary arcane, crucible and infusion recipes.
