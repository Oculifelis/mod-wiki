# Thaumic Exploration

Thaumic Exploration is a Thaumaturge port of [Thaumic Exploration](https://github.com/GTNewHorizons/Thaumic_Exploration) by Flaxbeard and contributors, in the GTNewHorizons fork. It adds wand cores and caps, rings, binding seals, urns and a set of soul machines.

**Requires [Thaumaturge](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1628024) 1.1.0 or newer.**

## What packs can change

Datapacks can change recipes, research and the data maps for the Thaumic Replicator, Soul Brazier, Crucible of Souls, Everburn Urn, Everfull Urn, Think Tank, Talisman of Nourishment, boot effects, worn and Curios attributes, ward and hurt-summon items, and the wand caps and rods.

## What it adds

The Thaumic Exploration research tab has about 30 entries. New wand parts are the Amber, Transmutative, Baguette and Necromancer's cores, in wand and staff sizes, and the Mechanist's and Sojourner's caps.

Worn items include six primal rings, the Thaumostatic Stabilizer, the Wispy Dreamcatcher and the Ring of the Taintacle. The Talisman of Nourishment eats food from the inventory. The Boots of the Comet and Boots of the Meteor are back.

Bound Chests and Bound Jars link through chest, jar and blank seals in sixteen colors. The Everburn Urn draws vis for lava, and the Jar of Oblivion voids what it is given. Sixteen colors of Floating Candle light a room.

Machines include the Thaumic Replicator, the Soul Brazier, the Crucible of Souls and the Think Tank, which turns books into research.

## Changed from the original

- Item state is stored in data components, and tunable numbers are data maps.
- The Everfull Urn is Thaumaturge's own urn, changed to run the original behavior.
- Discount rings apply to every aspect.
- The Talisman of Nourishment works anywhere in the inventory, as the original did.
- Wand parts use Thaumaturge's rod and cap registries.
- The debug commands and the parts that were commented out in the original are cut.
