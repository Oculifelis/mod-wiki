# Arcane Accretions

Arcane Accretions is a Thaumaturge port of [Thaumic Additions: Reconstructed](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/232564) by Zeitheron. It adds nine aspects, three new metals, big jars and void tools. It needs no HammerLib.

**Requires [Thaumaturge](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1628024) 1.1.0 or newer.**

## What packs can change

Datapacks can change recipes, research, item aspects, machine and gear numbers, seal combinations, Vis Salt effects, Beheader heads and smeltery stats.

## What it adds

A tab of 56 entries covers mithrillium, adaminite and mithminite, crystal machines and six jars up to mithminite. Food seasoned with Vis Salt gives an effect for each aspect. There are also Arcane Seals, void tools, The Beheader, the Essentia Pistol and the Entity Cell.

## Changed from the original

- It uses vis crystals, and baubles go in Curios slots.
- Machine numbers are datapack JSON.
- Caeles keeps the original's base red, since Thaumaturge aspects have one colour.
- Connected textures are gone, and the Amber Lamp no longer flickers.

## Optional mods and limits

[JEI](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/238222) shows Flux Concentrator conversions and crystal aspect variants. [Curios](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/309927) gives the baubles their slots. German, Russian and Simplified Chinese translations are included.

Parts that Thaumaturge or other ports already cover are cut, such as the Tome of Sharing, Shadow Enchanter, Void Anvil and Chester.
