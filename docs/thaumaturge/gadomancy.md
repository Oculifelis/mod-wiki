# Gadomancy

Give golems more ways to work, automate item handling and essentia storage, and build around aura nodes. My port brings Gadomancy to Thaumaturge with its own research tab. It also adds a summoned familiar, wearable gear and potion research.

The book divides the workshop, golem and aura research into separate entries, so use it to follow the prerequisites and see the recipes.

## Versions and requirements

Requires [Thaumaturge](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1628024) 1.1.0 or newer. [Curios](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/309927) 15 or newer is also required.
