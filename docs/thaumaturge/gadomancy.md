# Gadomancy Awakened

Gadomancy Awakened is a Thaumaturge port of [Gadomancy](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/237271) by makeo and HellFirePvP. It adds Silverwood golems, the Essentia Compressor, Aura Pylons and node tools.

**Requires [Thaumaturge](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1628024) 1.1.0 or newer and [Curios](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/309927) 15 or newer.**

## What packs can change

Datapacks can change recipes, research, Aura Pylon effects, Node Manipulator outcomes, what Aura Cores attune to and the shelves the Book of Knowledge reads.

## What it adds

The research tab has 28 entries. Silverwood golems, a Tooled Breaking seal, golem accessories and Sticky and Remote Jars build on Thaumaturge's golems and essentia. The Essentia Compressor stores huge amounts of essentia, and Aura Pylons breathe an aspect's effect over an area. The Node Manipulator reshapes nodes, and the Portal Focus opens a private eldritch maze. The Ethereal Familiar, the Block Protector, armour disguises and four new effects round it out.

## Changed from the original

- Golems use seals and golem accessories in place of golem cores.
- Baubles are Curios items.
- The Book of Knowledge gives theory knowledge.
- Hooks for mods with no 26.1 version are cut.
