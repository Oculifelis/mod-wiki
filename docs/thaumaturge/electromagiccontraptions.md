# Electro Magic Contraptions

Electro Magic Contraptions is a Thaumaturge port of [Electro-Magic Tools](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/236669) by Tombenpotter and TehNut. It adds electric tools and armour, essentia generators and solar panels. Its power is Forge Energy, so it works with other Forge Energy mods.

**Requires [Thaumaturge](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1628024) 1.1.0 or newer.**

## What packs can change

Datapacks can change recipes, research and the numbers for tools, armour, charging rings, spell effects, generators, solar panels and machines.

## What it adds

A research tab of 53 entries covers electric drills, chainsaws, omnitools and armour from Electric up to Quantum, plus four kinds of wings. Essentia generators turn six aspects into power, and the Industrial Wand Charging Station turns power back into vis. Compressed solars come in three tiers and six aspects. There are also six spell effects, Mjolnir and The One Ring.

## Changed from the original

- Power is Forge Energy, 8 FE to the old EU.
- Foci are now spell effects.
- The Quantum Chestplate has no modes.
- The Portable Node and the solar tiers above triple are cut.

## Optional mods and limits

With [FTB Industrial Contraptions](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/539097) installed, recipes use its circuits, batteries and machines. [JEI](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/238222) shows an Essentia Generator category and info pages. With [Curios](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/309927) installed, the charging rings go in a ring slot.
