# Thaumaturgic Bases

Grow magical plants, make new wand parts and wearable gear, and extend the alchemical workshop. My port of Thaumic Bases adds its own research tab to Thaumaturge, with decorative blocks and node research alongside the equipment.

The research book holds the recipes and their prerequisites. DummyCore is not required by this port.

## Versions and requirements

Requires [Thaumaturge](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1628024) 1.1.0 or newer.
