# Thaumaturgic Bases

Thaumaturgic Bases is a Thaumaturge port of [Thaumic Bases](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/232192) by Modbder. It adds Thauminite gear, magical crops and trees, node foci and the Revolver.

**Requires [Thaumaturge](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1628024) 1.1.0 or newer.**

## What packs can change

Datapacks can change recipes, research, node foci, revolver upgrades, tobacco and spike values, armour vis discounts and campfire logs.

## What it adds

The research tab has 112 entries. Thauminite tools and armour, new wand rods, crystal blocks and an alchemy chain for duplicating and breaking down ores come first. Magical crops, four special trees, the Thaumium Alchemical Furnace, two anvils and the Overchanting Table follow. It also adds tobacco and pipes, spikes, five enchantments, 12 spell effects, Casting Bracelets, node foci and the Revolver with 15 upgrades.

## Changed from the original

- Research points straight at Thaumaturge's own entries.
- Crystal shards are now vis crystals.
- The Peaceful Sapling stops at 12 animals within 8 blocks.
- Old vanilla-look blocks, Concentrated Taint and the poisoned revolver upgrade are cut.
