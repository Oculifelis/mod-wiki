# Thaumic Wonders

Thaumic Wonders is a Thaumaturge port of [Thaumic Wonders](https://github.com/daedalus4096/ThaumicWonders) by daedalus4096. It adds alchemy tools, strange devices, the Primordial Accelerator and gear such as the Primal Destroyer and the Flying Carpet.

**Requires [Thaumaturge](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1628024) 1.1.0 or newer.**

## What packs can change

Datapacks can change recipes, research, the soil each crop grows on, the structures a Structure Diviner finds, the blocks under a Void Beacon, the ores it conjures, the three multiblock blueprints and the extra drops from eldritch clusters.

## What it adds

Alchemy adds the Everburning Urn, the Alkahest Vat, Hexamite, Panacea, Lethe Water, the Flux Capacitor and Distiller, and the Creative Essentia Jar. Shimmerleaf, Cinderpearl and Vishroom grow as crops.

Devices include the Dimensional Ripper, the Inspiration and Madness Engines, the Meteorb, the Ore and Structure Diviners, the Void Beacon and the Timewinder.

The Catalyzation Chamber makes eldritch clusters from ore. The Primordial Accelerator, the Primordial Accretion Chamber and the Coalescence Matrix cover the later stages.

Gear covers the Primal Destroyer, the Flying Carpet, Void Fortress armour, Night-Vision Goggles, the Cleansing Charm, the Bow of Bone and six Primal Arrows. The Portal Anchor, Portal Generator and Void Portal lead to the Avatar of Corruption fight.

## Changed from the original

- Each feature is rebuilt on the closest Thaumaturge mechanic.
- The Inspiration and Madness Engines work through the research table, since Thaumaturge has no theorycraft cards.
- Baubles are Curios items.
- Vis crystals are Thaumaturge essentia crystals.
- Shimmerleaf, Cinderpearl and Vishroom are new crop blocks.
