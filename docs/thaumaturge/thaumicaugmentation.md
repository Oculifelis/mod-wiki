# Thaumic Augmentation

Thaumic Augmentation is a Thaumaturge port of [Thaumic Augmentation](https://github.com/TheCodex6824/ThaumicAugmentation) by TheCodex6824. It adds wards, impetus machines, rift studies, the Emptiness dimension, the Eldritch Spire and flight and weapon gear.

**Requires [Thaumaturge](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1628024) 1.1.0 or newer.**

## What packs can change

Datapacks can change every gameplay number through tuning files, the slots and augments of each host item, the Eldritch Altar ritual, which key opens each Eldritch Lock, how far an Autocaster reaches, fracture terrain and monitor colors, flux share by biome and the Primordial Wisp attacks.

## What it adds

A research tab leads through the whole mod. Ancient stone, fortified glass, the Vis Regenerator and the Robes of the Adept come first. Warding adds the Warded Chest, arcane doors, trapdoors, buttons and plates, and ward keys.

Impetus machines include the jewel, relay, gate, mirror, diffuser, matrix, generator and recharge pedestal. Rift studies add the Flux Inducer, the Stability Field Generator, the Reinforced Jar and the Primordial Wisp.

The Emptiness is a new dimension with three biomes, reached through Dimensional Fractures that the Fracture Locator finds. The Eldritch Spire holds locks, obelisks, an altar ritual, bosses, urns and Tattered Scrolls.

Gear includes Thaumium and Voidseer Caster's Gauntlets with augments, the Elytra Harness, the Impulse Cannon, the Morphic Tool, the Primal Cutter and the Ancient Focus. Control Seal: Secure, the Seal Copier, the Autocaster, the Celestial Observer, the Arcane Terraformer and the Biome Focus finish the set.

## Changed from the original

- Caster's Gauntlets are wands with fixed parts, since Thaumaturge casters are wands.
- Outer Lands sites, the eldritch guardian, warden and golem, flux rifts and wards use Thaumaturge's own versions.
- Item data lives in data components, not NBT.
- The Voidseer gauntlet's wide aura draw is cut.
