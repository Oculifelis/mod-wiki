# Arcane Locomotion

Arcane Locomotion is a Thaumaturge addon that adds magic crowbars, an essentia-fired steam locomotive, essentia freight carts and eldritch rails for Railcraft Reborn. It brings back the Thaumcraft module of old Railcraft with new code and textures.

**Requires [Thaumaturge](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1628024) 1.1.0 or newer and [Railcraft Reborn](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/901491) 1.4.5 or newer.**

## What packs can change

Datapacks can change recipes, research, the aspects of Railcraft items and carts, booster and eldritch rail tuning, spectral rail lifetime and the Aura Locomotive's vis use.

## What it adds

A Railcraft tab of 12 research entries starts with Thaumium and Void Metal Crowbars, and Railcraft's items and carts get aspects. The Thaumic Steam Locomotive burns Ignis and boils Aqua, and the Aura Locomotive draws vis from the chunk. Essentia Tank Carts fill at Essentia Loaders, Golem Stations hold carts for seals, and Rune Signals read a cart's aspects. There are also booster tracks, spectral rails, eldritch rails and six building sets.

## Optional mods and limits

[JEI](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/238222) shows info pages for the new items. The Thaumic Steam Locomotive is filled by hand from jars and phials. Railcraft content that Railcraft Reborn no longer has gets no aspects.
