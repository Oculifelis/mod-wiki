# Arcana Naturae

Arcana Naturae is a Thaumaturge port of [Magia Naturalis](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1365176) by Elenterius. It adds area sickles, Arcane Chests, Evil Trunks and the Phenomorphous Stone.

**Requires [Thaumaturge](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1628024) 1.1.0 or newer.**

## What packs can change

Datapacks can change the Phenomorphous Stone's block cycles, recipes and research.

## What it adds

The research tab has 20 entries. Thaumium and Void Sickles reap crops in an area, and the Sickle of Abundance multiplies harvests. The Phenomorphous Stone cycles blocks through their variants, and the Quicksilver Stone strengthens active effects. It also adds greatwood and silverwood Arcane Chests, four Evil Trunks that follow their owner with 36 slots each, the Prison Jar, thaumium keys and the Geo Pylon, which repaints biomes.

## Changed from the original

- Sickle reach is a radius with a block cap.
- Construction and Revenant are spell effects.
- Crashes, uneven sickle breaks and chests losing items are fixed.
- Taintman, Taint Breeder and the developer tools are cut.
