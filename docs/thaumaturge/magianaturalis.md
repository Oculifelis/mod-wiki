# Arcana Naturae

Harvest with sickles, work with mutations and quicksilver, and keep supplies in magical storage. My port of Elenterius’s Magia Naturalis adds its research to Thaumaturge, alongside carved woods, revealing glasses and ways to manage research.

Start in its book tab and follow the research for the part of your workshop you want to expand. The recipes and research requirements are in the book.

## Versions and requirements

Requires [Thaumaturge](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1628024) 1.1.0 or newer.
