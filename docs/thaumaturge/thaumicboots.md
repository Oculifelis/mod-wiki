# Thaumic Boots

Thaumic Boots is a Thaumaturge port of [Thaumic Boots](https://github.com/GTNewHorizons/ThaumicBoots) by Alastors. It adds 26 pairs of boots that run faster, jump higher, step up blocks and cancel falls.

**Requires [Thaumaturge](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1628024) 1.1.0 or newer.**

## What packs can change

Datapacks can change everything a pair of boots does through the `boot_stats` data map, which holds speed, jump, step height, fall rules, vis discount, warp, energy use and each special power. They can also change the aspects of the boots and the block and item tags the powers and upgrade recipes read.

## What it adds

Sixteen pairs come from the original, including Boots of the Sloth, the Seasons and Christmas Spirit, and the comet, meteor and voidwalker lines with electric, nano and quantum versions. Each has keybind modes for speed, jump, omni-movement, step assist and inertia canceling. The Arcanium Lens and a research tab with two new aspects, Caelum and Tabernus, go with them.

Ten pairs are new, each with one power.

- Groundline Striders drink FE from energy blocks underfoot.
- Blastcatch Boots of the Meteor store part of the explosion damage they take as FE.
- Framewalker's Boots of the Void let a crouch jump pass through frame blocks.
- Verdant Boots of the Seasons grow crops and saplings around the feet.
- Briarcatch Boots of the Sloth catch a fall in briars and root nearby mobs.
- Boots of the Grim Stomp hurt and wither mobs landed on.
- Lustral Boots of the Seasons turn taint blocks back and drain flux from the chunk.
- Primordial Boots of the Rift give two extra jumps in midair.
- Boots of Slothful Sin slow hostile mobs nearby.
- Spiritstep Boots turn the wearer invisible after a second of sneaking.

## Changed from the original

- Electric boots run on FE.
- Keybind modes are stored in a data component.
- The config modules are gone. Recipes and research switch on when the other mod is present.
- Ring items and stubs that the original never registered are cut.

<summary>Optional mods and limits</summary>

Electro Magic Contraptions, Tainted Magic and Thaumic Exploration add input boots for the upgrade recipes. Most of the new boots need another mod for their recipe, such as Fieldframe Arcana, Flora Ferox, Witching Gadgets, Forbidden Magic or Occultism. The Grim Stomp and Lustral boots need Thaumaturge only.
