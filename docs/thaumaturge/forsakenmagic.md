# Forsaken Magic

Forsaken Magic is a Thaumaturge port of [Forbidden Magic](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/224237) by Spiteful_Fox. It adds vice aspects and shards, dark enchantments, the Wrath Cage and morphic tools.

**Requires [Thaumaturge](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1628024) 1.1.0 or newer.**

## What packs can change

Datapacks can change recipes, research, the eight enchantments, wand core tuning, shard drops, the Heretic's trades and the spell parts.

## What it adds

A Forbidden research tab of 80 entries and seven vice aspects, each with its own shard. There are tainted, infernal and profane wand cores, morphic tools and the Axe of the Skulltaker. Eight dark enchantments, such as Wrath and Capitalist, are made by infusing books. The Wrath Cage, the Hellblink spell effect, taint trees and the Heretic villager round it out.

## Spell parts

Version 0.13.0 adds fourteen spell parts for the wand. Each has its own research entry, and its numbers are datapack JSON.

- Disarm knocks the held item out of a creature's hand.
- Decoy turns every nearby monster against the target, which glows for a while.
- Inverse turns the target's effects into their opposites, such as speed into slowness.
- Soulfire burns with soul flame that ignores armour and lights soul fire on soul sand.
- Return pulls a creature or dropped item back to the caster.
- Gravitas drags nearby creatures and items to where the spell lands and slows them.
- Siphon drains the target's life and heals the caster by half of it.
- Crushing ignores armour, wears it down, and grinds stone into cobblestone, gravel and sand.

Six more load only with another mod installed.

- Warp Transfer (Warp Theory) pushes the caster's temporary warp onto another player, or hurts a monster with it.
- Tithe (Sanguine Revelations) is a modifier that trades the caster's health for a stronger spell. Crimson Cult robes lower the price.
- Hexblight (Tainted Magic) spills the caster's warp onto the target as afflictions.
- Spirit Reap (Malum) makes creatures it kills drop their spirits.
- Mana Drain (Botania) pulls mana from a player's mana items into the caster's own.
- Life Essence (Blood Magic or Neo Vitae) is a modifier that spends soul network LP for a stronger spell.

## Changed from the original

- Neo Vitae replaces Blood Magic, and Ars Magica Legacy replaces Ars Magica 2.
- The Blink Focus is the Hellblink spell effect.
- Botania, Equivalent Exchange, Mob Factory Reloaded and Special Mobs parts are cut.

## Optional mods and limits

Neo Vitae adds Bloody Scrivener's Tools, the Rapier of the Strix, the Eldritch Blood Orb and a sanity ritual. Twenty other mods, such as Occultism and Witchery, add wand cores, caps, aspects and Occultism rituals. [JEI](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/238222) shows a Wrath Cage category.
