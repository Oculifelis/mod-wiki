# Forsaken Magic

Forsaken Magic is a Thaumaturge port of [Forbidden Magic](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/224237) by Spiteful_Fox. It adds vice aspects and shards, dark enchantments, the Wrath Cage and morphic tools.

**Requires [Thaumaturge](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1628024) 1.1.0 or newer.**

## What packs can change

Datapacks can change recipes, research, the eight enchantments, wand core tuning, shard drops and the Heretic's trades.

## What it adds

A Forbidden research tab of 80 entries and seven vice aspects, each with its own shard. There are tainted, infernal and profane wand cores, morphic tools and the Axe of the Skulltaker. Eight dark enchantments, such as Wrath and Capitalist, are made by infusing books. The Wrath Cage, the Hellblink spell effect, taint trees and the Heretic villager round it out.

## Changed from the original

- Neo Vitae replaces Blood Magic, and Ars Magica Legacy replaces Ars Magica 2.
- The Blink Focus is the Hellblink spell effect.
- Botania, Equivalent Exchange, Mob Factory Reloaded and Special Mobs parts are cut.

## Optional mods and limits

Neo Vitae adds Bloody Scrivener's Tools, the Rapier of the Strix, the Eldritch Blood Orb and a sanity ritual. Twenty other mods, such as Occultism and Witchery, add wand cores, caps, aspects and Occultism rituals. [JEI](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/238222) shows a Wrath Cage category.
