# Crimson Revelations

Crimson Revelations is a Thaumaturge port of [New Crimson Revelations](https://github.com/Thaumic-Modding-Team/NewCrimsonRevelations) by IcarussOne and the Thaumic Modding Team, a continuation of MobiusFlip's Crimson Revelations. It adds crimson cult gear, archers, new spell parts and a research tab about the Crimson Cult.

**Requires [Thaumaturge](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1628024) 1.1.0 or newer.**

## What packs can change

Datapacks can change recipes, research, the heads the Beheading enchantment takes, the three enchantments, the Thaumaturge villager's trades, loot additions, Tainted Lands spawns and the spell parts.

## What it adds

The Revelations research tab opens from Thaumaturge's crimson rites. Cultist knights, clerics and praetors drop Crimson Fabric and Crimson Plate, which make the Crimson Cult Sword, the Axe of Execution, the Pickaxe of Warped Distortion and recipes for Thaumaturge's cult robes, plate armour and Praetor armour.

Crimson Archers join the cult's portals and fire primal arrows when Thaumic Wonders is installed. Their hauberk, hood and greaves can be worn.

Beheading, Chameleon and Vis Attunement are infusion enchantments. Blinding Flash, Hex and Punch are new spell parts. Crimson Plate, Flesh and Magic Tallow are new golem materials. Four kinds of scribing tools, runic rings, amulets and girdles, and the Verdant Heart Band are added as well.

A Thaumaturge villager with its own tower trades for the new materials.

With [Thaumic Augmentation](thaumicaugmentation.md) installed, the Ancient Crimson, Crimson Paladin and Crimson Ranger armour sets appear, along with extra Tainted Lands spawns, the Overgrown Taintacle and the Taint Poison spell part.

## Changed from the original

- Every texture and model is new, built on vanilla and Thaumaturge art. The research text is rewritten.
- Focus effects are spell parts.
- Baubles are Curios items.
- Shards are vis crystals.
- Content that Thaumaturge or another port already has is left out, such as the Bow of Bone, the comet boots and the Ethereal Bloom.
