# Primal Artifice

Primal Artifice is a Thaumaturge port of [Planar Artifice](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/409849) by Leppaisawesome. It adds alkimium, eight kinds of glass, the Aura Meter and Starving Chests.

**Requires [Thaumaturge](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1628024) 1.1.0 or newer and [Curios](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/309927) 15 or newer.**

## What packs can change

Datapacks can change recipes, research, mirror stability, Starving Chest reach, smeltery stats, Flawless Focus limits and the two enchantments.

## What it adds

The research tab has 27 entries. Alkimium and bismuth come from the crucible, and eight kinds of glass are taken whole with a Glass Cutter. The Aura Meter shows the chunk's vis and flux. Starving Chests pull in nearby items, and the Alkimium Centrifuge splits compound essentia into all of its parts. It also adds Alkimium Smelteries, Flawless mirrors, the Mirrored Jar and Amulet, and the Metaphize and Mirrored enchantments.

## Changed from the original

- The Caster's Gauntlet is a Bismuth wand cap.
- Metaphize and Mirrored are datapack enchantments.
- Each glass has one plain texture instead of connected textures.
- The config file is gone.
