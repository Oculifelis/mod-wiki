# Thaumaturgic Concilium

Thaumaturgic Concilium is a Thaumaturge port of [Thaumic Concilium](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1056309) by Ilya3000 and BreadX22. It adds 13 spell effects, crimson cultists, flux rifts and flesh alchemy.

**Requires [Thaumaturge](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1628024) 1.1.0 or newer, [Curios](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/309927) 15 or newer, [Thaumaturgic Tinkerer](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1802868) 0.9.3 or newer and [Forsaken Magic](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1802941) 0.7.2 or newer.**

## What packs can change

Datapacks can change recipes, research, Chained Rift recipes, rift openings, mob spawns, sin auras and the Thaumaturge's trades.

## What it adds

A research tab of 64 entries and 13 new spell effects, such as the Vis Conductor and Warp Extraction. Mad Thaumaturges, Burned Witches and other new mobs roam the world, and crimson cultists hold the Crimson Avanpost. Flux rifts open where magic goes wrong, and the Hex of Predictability tames them. Late game brings the Crucible of Flesh and the Causal Bouillon dimension.

## Changed from the original

- Golem cores are gone, and seals do their job.
- Shards are vis crystals.
- Original bugs are fixed, like rifts growing past their maximum size.
- The Botania parts and the Witchery imp deal are cut.

## Optional mods and limits

Witchery, Ars Magica Legacy, Neo Vitae, Gadomancy Awakened, Thaumic Horizons, Thaumaturgic Bases and [Automagia](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1796732) each add content, including five casting bracelets.
