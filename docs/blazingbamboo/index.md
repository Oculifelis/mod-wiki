# Blazing Bamboo (Unofficial)

![Blazing Bamboo logo](../assets/blazingbamboo_logo.png){ width="128" align=right }

A port of [Blazing Bamboo](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1030015) by PaddedShaman, with his artwork, recipes and
values kept. It is not made or supported by PaddedShaman; report port problems at
[Oculifelis/BlazingBamboo](https://github.com/Oculifelis/BlazingBamboo).

Blazing bamboo grows in crimson forests. Living stalks hurt creatures that touch them; rain and water put them out,
and dead stalks drop gunpowder. Ice under a plant stops it growing.

| Thing | What it does |
|---|---|
| Blazing bamboo | Two pieces make a blaze rod. One piece smelts 4 items (800 ticks), a bundle smelts 40 (8,000 ticks). |
| Lava raft, chest raft | Float on lava and protect the riders from it while afloat. Sinking the raft removes that protection. |
| Bamboo wood set | Bundles, stripped bundles, planks and mosaics, with stairs, slabs, fences, doors, buttons, pressure plates and signs. |
| Blazing stone set | Stone and bricks with stairs, slabs, walls and chiseled bricks. |

## Compatibility

| Mod | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Fabric API](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/306612) | Required on Fabric. |
| [Thaumaturge](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1628024) | Optional, NeoForge 26.1.2 and 1.21.1: aspects for scanning and the crucible (Ignis and Herba; a bundle counts as nine pieces). A data pack can change them. |

**Versions:** 1.0.2. NeoForge 1.21.1, 26.1.2, 26.2, 26.3; Fabric 26.1.2, 26.2, 26.3.
