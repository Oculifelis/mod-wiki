# Classic Foci

Three ready-made Thaumaturge foci bring back the Portable Hole, Excavation and Equal Trade from Thaumcraft 4. Research them in Auromancy and craft them at the arcane workbench from a focus already holding the matching spell part.

**Requires** [Thaumaturge](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1628024) 1.1.0 or newer. Version **0.1.1** is published for NeoForge 26.1.2 and 1.21.1. [Public downloads](https://www.curseforge.com/projects/1796720)

| Focus | What it does |
|---|---|
| Portable Hole | Opens a temporary passage with Touch and Rift. |
| Excavation | Channels a beam that breaks the blocks it touches. |
| Equal Trade | Uses Bolt and Exchange; sneak-use picks the block to exchange for. |

These are ordinary Thaumaturge foci, so the focal manipulator can still edit them. They do not have Thaumcraft 4's separate per-focus upgrade slots or its Dowsing upgrade.
