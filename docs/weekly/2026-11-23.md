# 23 November 2026

## Tempestas 0.2.0: Storm Watch

Released weather now looks like weather. Rain, snow and storms let out of a jar show a cloud layer with rain or snow
falling from it.

The new **Storm Glass** is a placeable block that forecasts rain and thunder. A comparator reads it, so a redstone
build can react before a storm arrives.

The new skies to catch are First Drops, Rainbow, Calm Before, Dry Thunder and Thundersnow.

A new charge release effect charges nearby creepers. Datapacks can now time skies by the weather. Two new advancements
are Weather Eye and Ahead of the Storm.

See the [Tempestas guide](../tempestas/index.md).

## Atramentum 0.5.0: sea words

Two new words come from the sea. **Tide** is left by fishing and found in shipwrecks. **Abyss** is found only in ocean
monuments. Shipwrecks and ocean monuments also hold old ink now, and four new sentences use Tide and Abyss.

The Lacuna compat in Atramentum now works with Lararium, the mod's new name.

See the [Atramentum guide](../atramentum/index.md).
